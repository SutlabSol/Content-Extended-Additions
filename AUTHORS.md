# Authors

## Art

- **The game's icon** (the Android app's launcher icon, `halo.exe`'s icon
  and the desktop windows' icon): by ORION (`port/assets/icon`).
- **The controller's A, B, X and Y buttons** (the menus' button icons and
  the HUD messages'): redraws by saulob (`port/assets/buttons`).
- **The high-res HUD** (its meters, icons, scopes and messages, and the
  weapons' reticles): redraws by MrBruh (`port/assets/hud`), based on
  reference photos by Aerocatia. The corrected rocket/sniper reticles and
  sniper zoom labels (2x, 10x) use artwork supplied by the project owner
  (`port/assets/hud/corrections`); original artist attribution is unverified.
- **The menus' mouse cursor** (the blue arrow the menus show for the
  mouse): by pugzysixtyfour (`port/assets/cursor`).

## Data

- **The Switch's shared shader keys** (`keys.dkk`, which the Switch's
  updater downloads so a console compiles the campaign's and the stock
  multiplayer maps' shaders from its first start): collected by puginatux.

## Third-party art

- **Voice chat's speaker icons** (who talks, and who is muted, in the lobby,
  on the scoreboard and in the game): `volume-2` and `volume-x` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, copyright (c) 2026 Lucide Icons and
  Contributors, under the ISC licence
  (`port/assets/icons/lucide/LICENSE`).
- **The Mods menu's warning icons** (a mod out of its requested order, and
  a mod that cannot be played): `triangle-alert` and `circle-x` from
  [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) 1.53.0, under the same licence.

- **The service ranks' insignia** (`port/assets/firefight/rank_insignia`):
  Halo 3's UNSC insignia, redrawn as vectors by Beorn and
  [shared with the community](https://forums.bungie.org/halo/archive29.pl?read=854391).
  The tour shields behind them (`port/assets/firefight/rank_shields`) come from
  Content Extended's rank icons; original artist attribution is unverified.

- **Additional controller button styles**: supplied by the contributor
  (`port/assets/controller_buttons`); original artist attribution is unverified.
